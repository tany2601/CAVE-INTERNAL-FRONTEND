User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

In compliance with Meta Platform rules and applicable data privacy standards, customers of Cave Mens Salon may request the permanent deletion of their personal data (including name, phone number, billing history, and WhatsApp messaging logs) stored within our system (cave.paperlighttech.com).

How to Request Data Deletion

You can submit a data deletion request using either of the following two methods:

  • Method 1: In-Person at Cave Mens Salon. Visit the reception desk at Cave Mens Salon and inform the salon manager or staff that you would like your phone number and customer profile removed from the digital billing and WhatsApp system. Provide the phone number associated with your receipts for verification.
  • Method 2: Via Email. Send an email to cavesalonindia@gmail.com with the subject line: Data Deletion Request - Cave Mens Salon. In the body of the email, please include:
    1. Your full name
    2. The phone number (with country code, e.g., +91) that received WhatsApp messages from us

What Happens After You Request Deletion

  1. Verification: We will verify that the request comes from the owner of the phone number.
  2. Processing Time: Within 7 business days of verification, our technical team (Paperlight Tech) and Cave Mens Salon administrators will permanently delete or anonymize your personal contact details, opt-in status, and messaging records from our production database.
  3. Confirmation: You will receive a confirmation via email or at the salon once the deletion is complete. (Note: Basic anonymized invoice totals without personal identifiers may be retained strictly where required by Indian tax and accounting laws.)

Quick Opt-Out Alternative

If you only wish to stop receiving promotional WhatsApp messages without deleting your past billing receipts, simply reply STOP to any WhatsApp message from Cave Mens Salon.
