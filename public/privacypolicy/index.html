Privacy Policy — Cave Mens Salon Billing & Messaging System

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

1. Overview & Service Relationship

This Privacy Policy applies to the automated billing, reporting, and WhatsApp communication platform hosted at cave.paperlighttech.com (the "Service"). The Service is operated on behalf of Cave Mens Salon (https://cavesalon.com) and is technically developed, hosted, and maintained by Paperlight Tech (https://paperlighttech.com), acting as an authorized technology service provider and data processor.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit Cave Mens Salon, complete a service, or interact with our official WhatsApp Business account, we collect and process the following information:

  • Contact Details: Customer name and phone number.
  • Billing & Service Records: Invoice details, itemized salon services, payment amounts, and billing timestamps.
  • Messaging & Preference Logs: WhatsApp message delivery statuses, opt-in confirmations, and opt-out (STOP) commands.
  • Internal Administrative Data: Aggregated daily, weekly, and monthly sales reports sent to authorized Cave Mens Salon management.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data strictly for legitimate business operations of Cave Mens Salon:

  • Transactional Receipts: Delivering digital bills and service receipts via WhatsApp upon bill closure.
  • Promotional Offers: Sending salon discounts, seasonal offers, and updates strictly to customers who have opted in.
  • Opt-Out Management: Immediately honoring STOP replies to suppress future promotional messages.
  • Business Reporting: Generating internal operational and financial summaries for Cave Mens Salon administrators.

4. WhatsApp Business Cloud API & Meta Platforms

Our messaging infrastructure uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. Message transmission and delivery statuses are processed in accordance with Meta's WhatsApp Business Data Processing Terms.

5. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. Data is accessed only by authorized Cave Mens Salon staff and Paperlight Tech system administrators for technical maintenance. All data is stored in encrypted databases with strict access controls and secure API authentication.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. For full instructions on removing your records, please visit our User Data Deletion Instructions page or contact us directly at cavesalonindia@gmail.com or in person at Cave Mens Salon.
